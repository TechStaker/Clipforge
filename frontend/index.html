Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about video processing with ClipForge.

ClipForge processes high-fidelity source files directly using FFmpeg to preserve full frame rates, crisp audio, and pristine resolution without stream throttling or quality degradation. This also ensures complete compliance with platform terms by ensuring creators process content they own or are authorized to produce.

The engine calculates the total duration using FFprobe. If your video is 10 minutes long (600 seconds), it generates exactly 20 clips of 30 seconds each. If the final remaining duration is less than 30 seconds (e.g. 15 seconds), the last clip will cleanly contain that remaining duration.

ClipForge supports MP4, MOV, MKV, and WebM files. The default upload ceiling is configured up to 1 GB (1024 MB), which can be customized by the server administrator via environment variables.

For privacy and server hygiene, every job is assigned a unique isolated directory and automatically deleted after 60 minutes. Be sure to download your clips or the ZIP package promptly.
